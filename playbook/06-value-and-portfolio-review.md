# 6. Value and the portfolio review

> **Status: outline.**

## What this section decides

How the value of AI work is measured in terms leadership cares about, and how the whole portfolio is reviewed each quarter to decide what to scale, change or stop.

## Questions to answer

- Which few KPIs do you report (for example adoption, hours saved, cost per task, quality, risk incidents)? Why these?
- How do you set a baseline before anything changes, and who signs off on it?
- How do you turn time saved into money without overstating it? Which hourly rate do you use?
- How do you count running costs (licences, model usage, maintenance time)?
- What happens when a tool speeds up one step and the bottleneck moves to the next?
- What does the quarterly review look like: who attends, what they see, which decisions are made?
- What are the rules for scaling, changing or stopping a case?
- How do you report bad news, such as a pilot that did not work?

## Questions a sceptical leadership team will ask

- "Are these hours saved real, or just theoretical?"
- "What is the return on what we spend on AI?"
- "Which of these should we stop?"
