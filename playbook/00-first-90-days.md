## Questions to answer

- Who do you meet in the first two weeks, in what order, and what do you ask each of them?
- What do you find out about how people already use AI on their own, and how?
- What do you deliberately not do in the first 30 days, even if people expect it?
- What makes a case good enough to be one of the first two or three pilots? Who owns each one?
- Which rules do you put in place early, before the full approval flow exists (see section 2)?
- What does leadership get at day 30, day 60 and day 90? One page, a meeting, a decision request?
- Which decisions do you need from leadership, and by when?
- What would make you say at day 90 that the first quarter worked?

## Questions a sceptical leadership team will ask

- "Why not just roll out Copilot to everyone and see what happens?"
- "What will we have to show for this in three months?"
- "What does this cost, and what do we stop doing to make room for it?"
