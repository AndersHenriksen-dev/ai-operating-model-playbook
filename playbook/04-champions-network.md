# 4. The champions network

> **Status: outline.**

## What this section decides

How AI spreads through people in each team instead of through a central function alone: who the champions are, what they do, what they get, and how the network is run.

## Write here

<!-- Aim for 400-700 words. -->

## Questions to answer

- How do you pick champions? Volunteers, nominated by managers, or both? What do you look for?
- How many per team, and how much of their time do they get? Who agrees to that time?
- What does a champion actually do each month? What should each one ship?
- How often does the network meet, and what happens at the meetings?
- What do champions get in return: early access, training, recognition, a line in their review?
- How do you share what one team built with the others?
- What happens when a champion leaves or loses interest?
- How do you measure whether the network works?
- How does the network work with the risk rules (section 2) and the intake pipeline (section 1)?

## Must include

- [ ] Selection and time allowance
- [ ] The monthly rhythm and what each champion ships
- [ ] Recognition
- [ ] How results are tracked and shared
- [ ] What happens when a champion leaves

## Questions a sceptical leadership team will ask

- "Won't this just take time away from people's real jobs?"
- "What stops champions building things that become unmaintained risks?"
- "Why not hire specialists instead?"
