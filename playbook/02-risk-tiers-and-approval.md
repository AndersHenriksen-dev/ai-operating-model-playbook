# 2. Risk tiers and approval

> **Status: outline.** Check the current EU AI Act timeline and guidance before writing; obligations phase in over several years and dates have been discussed for change.

## What this section decides

Which uses of AI are allowed straight away, which need approval and from whom, and how this maps to the EU AI Act and GDPR. The aim is rules light enough that people follow them.

## Questions to answer

- How many tiers do you need, and what puts a use case in each one (type of data, who sees the output, what the output may decide)?
- Which uses need no approval at all, so people can start today?
- Where must a person check the output before it is used, and where may AI act on its own?
- How do the tiers relate to the AI Act's risk categories (prohibited practices, high-risk systems, transparency obligations, minimal risk)?
- When is personal data involved, and when do you need a data protection impact assessment under GDPR?
- How are new AI tools and vendors approved, and who signs off on them?
- Who approves each tier: you, IT security, legal and compliance, the business owner?
- What is logged, and who reviews the rules, how often?
- How do you meet the AI Act's requirement that staff have sufficient AI literacy (Article 4)? Link to section 3.

## Must include

- [ ] The tier table with concrete examples from a 200-person company

- [ ] The mapping to the AI Act and GDPR, and a note on what is checked with legal counsel

## Questions a sceptical leadership team will ask

- "Are we exposed under the AI Act right now?"
- "Can employees put customer data into ChatGPT or Copilot?"
- "Who is accountable when an AI output is wrong?"
