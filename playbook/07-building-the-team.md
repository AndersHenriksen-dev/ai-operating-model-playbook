# 7. Building the team

> **Status: outline.**

## What this section decides

Who the first two hires are, in which order, how they are found and assessed, and how the small team works with the rest of the company.

## Write here

<!-- Aim for 400-700 words. -->

## Questions to answer

- Which two roles do you hire first, and why those two (for example a builder and an enablement or adoption lead)? What do you do yourself until they start?
- What must each hire be able to do in their first three months?
- Do you hire, borrow people from IT or the business, or use external help? When each?
- How do you assess candidates? What work sample or case do you use?
- How do you lead a small team of specialists: how much freedom, which priorities, how often do you meet?
- How does the team split work between building, teaching and governance?
- How does the team work with IT, security, legal and the champions network?
- What does the team look like after a year if things go well?

## Must include

- [ ] The first two roles, with a short profile each
- [ ] Hiring order and timing
- [ ] The assessment method
- [ ] How the team is led and how it works with the rest of the company

## Questions a sceptical leadership team will ask

- "Why do you need a team at all? Can't IT do this?"
- "What if we can only afford one hire?"
- "How do you keep the team from becoming a bottleneck?"
