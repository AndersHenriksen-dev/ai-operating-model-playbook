# 3. AI literacy by role

## What this section decides

What each group of employees must be able to do with AI, how they learn it, and how you know it worked.

## Questions to answer

- Which role groups do you train differently (for example everyone, power users and champions, developers, managers, legal and compliance)?
- For each group, what must they be able to do after training? Write it as abilities, not topics.
- Which formats do you use (live workshops, one-to-one sessions, written guides, recorded material), and why for which group?
- How do you make training about people's own work instead of generic demos?
- What do managers need to know to lead teams that work with AI?
- How do you check that training changed how people work, not just that they attended?
- How does this cover the AI Act's AI literacy requirement (see section 2)?
- How do you keep it current as tools and models change every few months?

## Questions a sceptical leadership team will ask

- "How many hours of people's time will this take?"
- "Our people already use ChatGPT at home. Why train them?"
- "How do we know the training was worth it?"
